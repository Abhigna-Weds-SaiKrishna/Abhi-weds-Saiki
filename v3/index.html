Traditions

Sacred Telugu Rituals

Centuries-old customs binding two souls and two families in divine companionship

01 / Muhuratham

Jeelakarra Bellam

At exactly 9:22 AM, the couple places cumin and jaggery on each other's crowns, signifying an inseparable bond of sweetness and strength.

02 / Sacred Tie

Mangalasutra Dharana

The groom ties the sacred golden Mangalasutram around the bride's neck with three sacred knots representing body, mind, and soul.

03 / Celebration

Talambralu

An exuberant, joyful shower of turmeric-infused pearls and rice poured by the bride and groom over one another amidst songs and laughter.

04 / Eternal Vow

Saptapadi

Seven sacred steps around the holy fire (Agni), exchanging seven timeless promises of nourishment, courage, loyalty, and companionship.
